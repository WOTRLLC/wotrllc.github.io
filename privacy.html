WisdomOTR Reels Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

WisdomOTR Reels is an internal tool run by Wisdom on the Rock LLC. It uploads short videos to the company's own YouTube channel (@WISDOMOTR) after the channel owner approves each one.

What it accesses. With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API upload permission (youtube.upload) to upload videos to that one channel. It does not read, change, or delete anything else in the Google account.

What it stores. Each uploaded video's ID and publish time, kept in a private database on the company's server. It collects no personal information about viewers or anyone else.

Sharing. We do not sell or share any data. Uploads go to YouTube and fall under the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access. The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
