WisdomOTR Reels Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

WisdomOTR Reels is an internal tool run by Wisdom on the Rock LLC. It manages short videos on the company's own YouTube channel (@WISDOMOTR). Each video is approved by the channel owner before it is scheduled.

What it accesses. With the channel owner's permission, the app uses these Google permissions, only for the company's own channel:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): uploads approved videos and sets their publish time.
  • Manage the channel's videos (youtube.force-ssl): reads the channel's video list and public counts (views, likes, comments), corrects a video's title, description or tags, and changes a scheduled video's publish time or keeps it private. It does not delete videos.
  • View YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly): reads channel-level and per-video totals such as views, watch time and average view duration, to see which videos do well.

It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in the Google account, and it does not act on any other channel.

What it stores. Video IDs, titles, publish times and the channel's summary statistics and analytics totals, kept in a private database on the company's server. YouTube Analytics data is aggregated by YouTube; the app does not receive or collect personal information about viewers or anyone else.

Sharing. We do not sell or share any data. Uploads go to YouTube and fall under the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Revoking access. The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
